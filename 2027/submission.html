Paper Tracks

There are two paper tracks available for AsHES'27:

  • 1) Full paper track (10 pages) including citations;
  • 2) Short paper track (maximum of 4 pages) including citations; meant to highlight early investigations of innovative ideas and position papers on emerging topics. Authors of accepted short papers will present their work both as a short talk and as a poster in the IPDPS cross-workshop poster session.

Submitted papers will undergo a single-blind review process, so the authors do not need to anonymize a submission. Every submission will receive at least three reviews. Papers are submitted through Linklings. The submission form asks how you learned of AsHES; the answer is used only to improve our publicity and is not visible to reviewers.

Submission site: To be announced

Poster Session

AsHES'27 takes part in the IPDPS 2027 cross-workshop poster session, held on the evening of the workshop day (June 1, 6:00-8:00 pm). Posters are a required element of the short-paper track, and authors of all accepted full papers are strongly encouraged to present a poster as well, as the session offers a longer window for discussion than a talk permits. Details will be provided in the acceptance notifications and the camera-ready instructions.

In-Person Presentation Requirement

At least one author of each accepted paper must register for IPDPS 2027 and present the paper in person in Seattle. All workshop chairs will attend in person. Any request for remote accommodation will be referred to the IPDPS Workshop Chairs; the AsHES organizers cannot grant it.

Visa Letters

The submission form includes a checkbox asking whether an author anticipates needing a visa letter to attend. The response is not visible to reviewers and plays no part in the decision on the paper. Authors who request a letter are expected to apply for their visa promptly and to be able to provide proof of application.
